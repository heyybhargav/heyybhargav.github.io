Side project

Siftl

An AI-powered content-curation SaaS built from scratch.

I built Siftl single-handedly to solve my own problem of finding and organizing high-quality online writing, running a content-curation pipeline continuously without racking up hosting or database fees.

  • Serverless data parsing: Node.js CRON tasks that fetch, digest, and filter 10,000+ online data points daily.
  • Zero-cost stack: engineered the backend inside free-tier limits, proving scalable software doesn't need a big cloud budget.
  • Algorithmic curation: simple, effective NLP heuristics to categorize and rank articles by relevance and information density.

Siftl was a playground for mastering background scheduling, scraping defenses, and clean database structures. Live at siftl.com.

AI learning companion

Acharya

Learn anything from any video, podcast, or lecture.

Acharya acts as a personal tutor for online video and audio. By ingesting YouTube lectures, podcasts, or local audio uploads, it constructs a fully interactive classroom.

  • Content ingestion pipelines: handlers that scrape, transcribe, and index raw YouTube links or direct audio files.
  • Interactive study loops: context-aware prompting that generates notes, flashcards, quizzes, and Q&A on demand.
  • Minimalist interface: a clean, lightweight UI focused on video consumption alongside rapid Q&A.

Acharya lowers the barrier to active recall from long-form educational video. Try it at myacharya.vercel.app.

Ribbit Capital's June 2025 tokenized-economy thesis.

An interactive explainer synthesizing Ribbit Capital's June 2025 letter. It breaks down the taxonomy of stablecoin settlement pipelines, proprietary data-token factories, and context flywheels.

  • Machine-legibility taxonomy: modeled Access, Memory, Expert, Context, Identity, Knowledge, and Asset tokens.
  • Interactive data interfaces: reactive charts rendering inference-cost collapses (1,000x over three years) and settlement-volume thresholds.
  • Gamified retention loops: a client-side quiz testing users on data moats and agentic resource allocation.

It maps the transition where vertical token networks dismantle traditional BPO and software ERPs. Explore it at thetokenrevolution.vercel.app.

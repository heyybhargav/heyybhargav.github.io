Side Project

Siftl

An AI-powered content curation SaaS built from scratch.

Core Execution & Architecture

I built Siftl single-handedly to solve my own problem of finding and organizing high-quality online writings. The goal was to run a robust content curation pipeline continuously without racking up hosting or database fees.

  • Serverless Data Parsing: Structured Node.js CRON tasks configured to fetch, digest, and filter over 10,000 online data points daily.
  • Zero-Cost Stack: Engineered the backend inside zero-cost infrastructure limits, proving that scalable software doesn't require massive initial cloud budgets.
  • Algorithmic Curation: Built simple, effective NLP heuristic layers to categorize and rank articles by relevance and information density.

Siftl served as a playground for mastering the details of background scheduling, web scraping defenses, and clean database structures. You can visit the live project at siftl.com.

AI Learning Companion

Acharya

Learn anything from any video, podcast, or lecture.

Core Execution & Architecture

Acharya is designed to act as your personal tutor for online video and audio media. By ingestion of YouTube lectures, podcasts, or local audio uploads, the system constructs a fully interactive classroom.

  • Content Ingestion Pipelines: Built ingestion handlers to instantly scrape, transcribe, and index raw YouTube video links or direct audio files.
  • Interactive Study Loops: Programmed context-aware prompting systems to generate custom notes, flashcards, interactive quizzes, and Q&A guides on-demand.
  • Minimalist Learning Interface: Crafted a clean, lightweight UI designed to focus on video consumption alongside rapid question-and-answer interactions.

Acharya lowers the barrier to active recall and learning from long-form educational videos. You can try the live application at myacharya.vercel.app.
